# Product scope implemented in this starter

## Customer
OTP registration, profile, discovery/search, public business detail, reviews, customer invoices, invoice-render download/regeneration request, wallet/coin experience, reports/messages foundation.

## Business owner
"Start your business" multi-step flow, public profile/branding, verification request, service/category catalog, inventory ledger, invoice wizard, payment methods, SMS policy, wallet/coins, staff account request, reports and plan/feature foundations.

## Platform admin
Dashboard, users/business freezes, feature states/overrides, plans, loyalty levels/coin rules, encrypted integration settings, SMS moderation, verification, staff requests, reports, and platform-level policy foundations.

## Public website
Marketing pages, directory, public business page with structured data, trust/eNamad slot, app screenshot skeletons and promotional-video placeholder.
